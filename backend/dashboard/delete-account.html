Account Deletion Request

At Finovert, we value your privacy and give you full control over your data. If you wish to delete your account and all associated personal data from our systems, please follow the instructions below.

How to request deletion:

Please send an email from your registered email address to:

support@finovert.com

Subject: Account Deletion Request - [Your Full Name]

What data will be deleted?

  • Your personal profile information (name, email, mobile number).
  • All saved documents and images uploaded to the platform.
  • Your transaction history and service requests.
  • Your device-specific identifiers and usage logs.

Note: Some data may be retained for a limited period if required by applicable financial regulations or legal obligations in India. Once processed, your account cannot be recovered.
